# Rulebook 2026 amendments (WA1500 statements)

Official WA1500 statements that are **not (fully) reflected** in `rulebook2026.txt` (Issue 2026-01-01). Where a statement and the rulebook disagree, the statement is listed here as the later or more specific source, but the board has not confirmed which prevails. Check upstream before relying on either.

- Source: <https://wa1500.org/rules_details.php?Thema=33>, retrieved 2026-10-27. PDFs are at `https://wa1500.org/file.php?ID=<id>`.
- Rule numbers below are the **2026** numbers. Statements issued before 2025 use the older numbering (old 3.3 Optical Sight, old 3.4 Rifle 1500, old 3.11/3.12 Standard Semi-Automatic 5,5"/3,56").
- Upstream wording and typos are kept as is.

## A. Missing from the rulebook

### A1. Rule 1.8(g) Re-Entry Match (ID 1326, 2022-03-29)

The rulebook has only the definition. The statement adds the limits:

- During one announced competition a shooter may shoot at most one Revolver 1500 match, and/or one Pistol 1500 match, and/or one Optical Sight match (the statement says "Lit. 3 will be changed in the new rulebook"; today this means Revolver OS and Pistol OS, rules 3.3 and 3.4).
- Valid for scheduled competitions of one or more days.
- If two competitions are scheduled in one weekend (Saturday and Sunday), all three match types may be shot on each day.
- Results must be reported separately for each match.
- Shooting e.g. two Revolver matches in one competition and choosing the best for ranking and classification is prohibited.

### A2. Rule 4.1 Official targets (ID 1388, 2023-08-18)

The rulebook only says "see statement #4.1". The statement:

- WA1500-27 and WA1500-27E are approved at the standard distances 3, 7, 15, 25 and 50 yards or meters. No rule change, only a clarification of which targets to use.
- WA1500-29 (reduced target) is also defined; Carbine 1500 requires it at all distances and stages.

### A3. Rule 3.8 Excluded firearms (ID 1431, 2024-06-27)

Technical Commission decision: these firearms do not conform to rule 3.8 (Standard Revolver 4,25"):

- Korth Ranger Revolver 4"
- S&W 686 Performance Center 4"

The listing "will be expanded when required".

### A4. Firearms according or not according to the rules (ID 1473, 2024-12-10)

Applies to rules 3.1–3.12. Authorized, since neither has barrel venting and both are gas-operated:

- Laugo Arms Alien
- Heckler & Koch P7

### A5. Rule 3.11 list of approved firearms (ID 1563, undated)

Rule 3.11 says the pistol "must be approved by the WA1500 and included into the list of approved firearms", but the rulebook has no list. The list is a separate PDF (12 pages, about 300 entries of manufacturer/model with caliber, mostly German-language names) titled "#3.11 approved firearms - preliminary list - do not use for investment". It is not reproduced here.

## B. In the rulebook, but conflicting or incomplete

### B1. Rule 3.17(a) maximum power (ID 1362)

- Statement: "Any ammunition factor higher than 180, is prohibited" (maximum power factor 180).
- Rulebook: "Maximal power 600 Joule. Any change to joule higher than 180, is prohibited." The 180 is attached to joule, which is not a coherent limit. Probably a mistranslation of the statement.

### B2. Rule 3.17(h) minimum power factor, 3,7" pistol (ID 1362)

- Statement (old 3.12, Standard Semi-Automatic 3,56"): no minimum power factor, only that the semi-automatic function must work properly. Old 3.11 (5,5"): minimum 120.
- Rulebook 3.17(h) applies "a minimum factor of 120" to every Standard Semi-Automatic Pistol, including 3.13 (3,7").

### B3. Rule 3.10 trigger (ID 1472)

- Statement: "May have single and double action capability. Hammerless action is allowed. No Trigger pull weight required." In effect from 2025-01-01, with the rulebook to be revised on 2026-01-01.
- Rulebook 3.10: "Must have single and double action capability ... Hammerless action is allowed." "Must" versus "May" changes whether a single-action-only or double-action-only revolver qualifies.
- Rules 3.8 and 3.9 also say "Must have single and double action capability"; the statement covers only 3.10.

### B4. Rules 3.3 and 3.4 Optical Sight (ID 1474)

Separation into Revolver OS and Pistol OS is incorporated (decided at the AGM on 2024-08-23, effective 2025-01-01). Missing or garbled:

- The clarification that the original iron sights may be removed to fit the scope or dot, or remain on the firearm with the scope or dot.
- Rule 3.4 reads "A Pistol according to 3.2 or a Pistol according to 3.2, except the sight." (duplicated clause).

### B5. Rule 20.2 Classification transition (ID 1474)

Statement transition from the combined Optical Sight classification:

- A shooter classified in Optical Sights uses that classification in the first Revolver OS match and the first Pistol OS match.
- A shooter classified in Revolver 1500 or Pistol 1500 uses that classification in the respective Revolver OS or Pistol OS match.
- Proceed as usual afterwards.

Rulebook 20.2 only says the classifications are separated "after the first match Revolver 1500 (Rule 3.1) or Optical Sight 1500 (Rule 3.3)" and does not describe the carry-over. The statement was valid until 2025-12-31.

### B6. Rule 3.6(f) Distinguished Revolver trigger stop (ID 1508, 2025-04-14)

The Technical Commission cancelled the ban on "any external trigger stop device". Reasons: the other revolver rules have no such restriction, it makes rule 3.6 logical in itself, a trigger stop is not effective in double action shooting, and a badly adjusted trigger stop causes problems in single action shooting.

- Statement new rule: "The following are not allowed: trigger shoes; compensator; use of any filler substance ...".
- Rulebook 3.6(f) still reads "trigger shoes; compensator; any external trigger stop device; use of any filler substance ...".
- The statement was in force 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 and said the rulebook would be revised on 2026-01-01; this change was not carried into the 2026 text.
- The statement text also differs slightly from the rulebook ("thumb rests or stocks flared at the base" and a trailing "barrel venting or barrel p"); the rulebook has neither.

## C. Expired or informational

### C1. Rule 3.11 decision on non-conforming pistols (ID 1537, 2025-07-17)

Pistols not in strict compliance with 3.11 had been approved by Gun Control at competitions including European and World Championships over the previous 2–3 years. To avoid punishing shooters who bought them in good faith, the Board (meeting 2025-07-07) decided they stay approved for the 2025 World Championship and until the end of 2025. A solution was to be offered before the end of 2025. The 2026 rulebook (3.11 requires the approved-firearms list, A5) is the follow-up; no further exemption is known.

## Incorporated, no action

### Trigger pull weights (ID 1361)

The 2023 table matches the 2026 rulebook: Revolver 1500 and Revolver OS 2 ½ lbs (1135 g) single action; Pistol 1500, Pistol OS and Standard Semi-Automatic 3 lbs (1360 g); Distinguished Revolver 2 ½ lbs (1135 g); Distinguished Pistol 3 lbs (1360 g); Standard Revolvers none required.
